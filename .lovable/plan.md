## Honest caveats

- No revenue or paid-subscription data yet — the product is not monetized.
- No formal review mechanism is installed, so there are no star-ratings or testimonials captured in-app.
- The 85% bounce rate is typical for a single-call-to-action landing page, but there is room to improve with a stronger first-visit hook.
